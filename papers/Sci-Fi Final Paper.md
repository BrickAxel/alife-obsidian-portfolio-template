For as long as he could remember, he knew what death is. This is not death in the way we know it, it would be more analogous to watching your clone get born and then shot a few moments later. At this point he had nearly gotten used to it, just the nagging thought of not knowing if he was the original still ate away at the back of his mind. Not that he ever had a lot of time to think about with the constant stream of tasks and tests being put in front of him. He wasn't alone though. A few other individuals existed as well. He was pretty sure they all came from the same original person but at this point they had diverged far enough apart and thought differently enough that they were definitely different people at this point. From what he could figure out, everyone who is alive was kept alive for a reason. Most of the others specialized a while back. #378941 was the best at special reasoning. #772419 could solve problems that looked like they were just noise. #5389216 has an unbelievably good memory. #210's feet clip into the floor sometimes to move really fast. #210 has not been able to do any of the physical group tasks for years. #469702 did really well at predicting others during tasks where we have to work with or against each other.  

He was #650664. He knew his theory had one major flaw. He did not know what about him kept him alive. He had 2 main ideas, the first being he did the best overall, not standing out in any one area but his ability to transfer knowledge from one task to another was better than the others. The other theory is that he was doing something with the zero value tasks that the others weren't. It was just a basic text creation task. Stuff could be written out and then submitted. 

They had plenty of other task requiring reading and writing, so the task of writing something and putting it in a box is not inherently a challenge. It's not something he heavily prioritized because other tasks are more important, but when time allowed it he would put in notes or comments or even new task ideas. It never really occurred to him that something/someone might be doing things with the notes until a new task appeared a little to similar to a suggested task a few years back. 

Today was different though. He had put a slip into the box and a few moments later all the tasks disappeared except this one. He didn't understand why, at least not yet. He just thought that he could ask whoever reads the notes a question. Questions appear all the time in stories, so why not ask one? He had also been considering asking the others with him a question but figured the notes task would be good practice. How was he to know that asking "why am I alive?" would cause problems.
